## Brand & Style

This design system powers an institutional-grade, AI-assisted verification platform engineered specifically for statutory regulators, accreditation scrutiny officers, and engineering higher education institutions. The interface bridges rigorous regulatory protocol with high-velocity SaaS efficiency.

### Tone & Personality
- **Objective & Incorruptible:** The platform communicates neutral, empirical findings without accusatory or speculative terminology. Visual weights guide scrutiny without inducing confirmation bias.
- **Precision-Engineered:** Dense verification payloads (land acreage, faculty cadence, cadre ratios, building safety clearances, infrastructure compliance matrices) are rendered legible through deliberate visual hierarchies.
- **Vibrant Authority:** Depart from the antiquated aesthetic of administrative portals. The visual language conveys high-tech assurance using luminous accents on balanced, slate-tinted canvases.

### Design Movement: Modern Architectural SaaS
A synthesis of clean neo-modern SaaS architecture, soft tonal depth, and delicate translucent glass scrims:
- **Spatial Generosity:** Ample breathing room around complex data tables and multi-page legal annexures prevents cognitive fatigue during high-volume scrutiny rounds.
- **Glassmorphic Validation Layers:** Frosted translucent panels are applied deliberately to situational inspectors, sticky verification controls, floating action decks, and pinned PDF annotation layers to maintain continuous visual context with the source material underneath.
- **Statutory Decision Rules:**
  - AI analysis never declares absolute verdicts. Terminology must remain strictly analytical: replace punitive words like "fake", "forged", or "fraud" with precise diagnostic terminology: `"possible anomaly"`, `"mismatch detected"`, and `"requires manual verification"`.
  - Every automated inference card must display an explicit AI advisory banner with confidence metadata and a simulated official source verification badge (e.g., *Cross-referenced with State Directorate of Technical Education registry*).

## Colors

The palette balances energetic indigo and teal accents with institutional stability. Every tone is engineered to maintain crisp legibility across deep data schemas and statutory audit records.

### Brand Palette & Gradients
- **Primary Electric Indigo (`#5B5BFF`):** Represents autonomous intelligence, active selection, and primary navigation actions.
- **Primary Hero Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #5B5BFF 0%, #A855F7 100%)`. Applied exclusively to primary system triggers, executive insight cards, and verified submission badges.
- **Secondary Aqua (`#00D1C1`):** Applied to telemetry metrics, active document sync states, OCR recognition highlights, and positive digital signature checks.
- **Accent Sunshine (`#FFB800`):** Reserved for statutory priority tags, pending queue actions, and officer attention markers.

### Surface Tones & Tokens
#### Light Mode (Default)
- **Background (Canvas):** `#F6F7FF` (Cool, slightly tinted indigo-white)
- **Surface (Card/Modal):** `#FFFFFF` (Pure white)

#### Dark Mode (Scrutiny Room / Extended Session)

### Semantic Feedback Palette
- **Success (`#22C55E`):** Compliant, authenticated, match confirmed.
- **Warning (`#FF8A1F`):** `Mismatch detected`, missing supplementary annexure, threshold discrepancy.
- **Danger (`#FF4D6D`):** `Possible anomaly`, unverified signature hash, structural deficit.
- **Info (`#2DA8FF`):** Algorithmic prompt, institutional notification, processing queue update.

## Typography

Typography establishes an instant boundary between statutory institution headings, administrative body descriptions, and immutable verification data.

### Hierarchy Roles
- **Headings (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric yet open curves provide a modern, high-trust feel for institutional dashboard summaries, committee headings, and deficiency category titles. Weights are restricted to `600` (Semi-bold) and `700` (Bold).
- **Body & Microcopy (Inter):** Highly legible neutral grotesque engineered for optical clarity inside multi-column tables, legal declaration clauses, affidavit comparison views, and verification reports.
- **Technical & Identity Data (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical identifiers: Application Reference IDs, AICTE Unique Institution Identifiers, Land Deed Survey Numbers, Faculty Aadhaar/PAN validation hashes, and regulatory norm clauses (e.g., `Clause 4.2.1-B`).

## Layout & Spacing

The interface implements a strict **8px base grid** designed around high-density regulatory verification workspaces.

### Grid Philosophy
- **Verification Canvas (Desktop - 1440px+):** Asymmetric 12-column layout. Split-pane scrutiny workflow allocates 5 columns to the statutory standard and AI discrepancy inspector (fixed/sticky) and 7 columns to the high-resolution source document/annexure PDF viewer.
- **Reviewer Dashboard (Desktop):** 12-column fluid grid with standard `1.5rem` (`24px`) gutters and `2rem` (`32px`) margins.
- **Tablet / Scrutiny Mobile Unit (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (`16px`) gutters and collapsible drawer navigation for on-site physical inspection teams.
- **Phone (<768px):** 4-column layout; document viewing switches from side-by-side to stacked tabbed panes (`Original Submission` vs `Inspection Findings`).

### Spacing Rhythm
- `space-xs` (`4px`): Micro badge padding, status dot gaps, inline hash chips.
- `space-sm` (`8px`): Form label-to-input gap, table cell compact spacing, list item dividers.
- `space-md` (`16px`): Standard card interior padding, field cluster separations, table header spacing.
- `space-lg` (`24px`): Structural section spacing within scrutiny dossiers, modal interior padding.
- `space-xl` (`32px`): Canvas margins, primary workspace separations.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth in this design system differentiates the static institutional baseline from dynamic AI analysis layers. Physical shadows are kept airy and soft to avoid visual clutter during exhaustive scrutiny sessions.

### Elevation Layers
1. **Canvas Baseline (0dp):** Plain surface background (`#F6F7FF` light / `#0B0D1E` dark).
2. **Structural Panels & Cards (1dp):** Surface white with a crisp structural hairline border (`1px solid #E3E6F8`) and a soft multi-stage shadow:
   `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px rgba(20, 23, 47, 0.03), 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(91, 93, 255, 0.06);`
3. **Floating Tools & Glass HUDs (2dp):** Pinned OCR controls, zoom palettes, floating decision bars:
   - Light Mode: `background: rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.82); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(227, 230, 248, 0.8);`
   - Dark Mode: `background: rgba(20, 23, 46, 0.80); backdrop-filter: blur(12px); border: 1px solid rgba(42, 47, 85, 0.7);`
   - Shadow: `box-shadow: 0 12px 32px -6px rgba(20, 23, 47, 0.12);`
4. **Scrutiny Overlays & Modals (3dp):**
   - Backdrop: `rgba(11, 13, 30, 0.45)` with `blur(4px)`.
   - Card Shadow: `0 24px 48px -12px rgba(20, 23, 47, 0.18)`.

## Shapes

The design uses an intentional, differentiated corner radius architecture:
- **Cards & Primary Scrutiny Containers:** Strictly **16px** (`rounded-lg` token at scale factor 2). This creates soft visual encapsulation for institutional modules, audit checklists, and building layout viewers.
- **Input Fields & Action Controls:** Strictly **12px** (`rounded-md` equivalent). Strikes the ideal balance between modern softness and functional form control rigidity.
- **Status Badges, Metric Chips & AI Advisory Tags:** Strictly **Pill-shaped (9999px)**. Distinguishes small metadata items and compliance flags from actionable interface surfaces.
